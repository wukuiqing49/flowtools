Privacy

Privacy by design.

FlowTools products are designed to perform useful work on your device and collect only what is necessary to provide and improve each application.

Our privacy principles

FlowTools develops professional Android applications with an offline-first approach. Core processing is designed to happen locally whenever practical. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use the content you process in our applications for advertising.

Information and app data

The information handled by a FlowTools product depends on the work it performs. This may include photos you choose, image metadata, inspection records, report content, location details or exported documents.

Files selected or created by an application remain under your control. A product may store preferences and working data locally on your device so that it can operate offline. Where an app uses optional diagnostics, the relevant app listing or in-app notice will explain what is collected and how it is used.

Device permissions

FlowTools applications request Android permissions only when a feature needs them. Depending on the product, these may include access to selected photos, the camera, storage or location. You can review and revoke permissions at any time in Android settings, though doing so may prevent related features from working.

Retention and security

Locally stored project information remains on your device until you remove it, clear the app data or uninstall the application. When limited service data must be retained, it is kept only for as long as needed for the stated purpose or legal requirements.

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. No system can guarantee absolute security, so we also design our products to minimize unnecessary data transfer and storage.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when products, legal requirements or our data practices change. The revision date at the top of this page identifies the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email support@example.com. Please name the FlowTools product involved so we can respond accurately.
