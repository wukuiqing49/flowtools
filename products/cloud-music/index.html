Product purpose

What Cloud Music helps you do

Cloud Music combines local tracks and user-selected cloud music sources in a straightforward Android library. It keeps common destinations such as artists, playlists and device music within a small number of navigation steps.

The app is intended for people who already manage their own music collection and want a focused player rather than a subscription catalog. Local scanning brings compatible files into the library, while cloud access remains a separate source chosen by the user.

Music already stored on the device can be organized and played through the local library without opening a cloud source. Network access is needed when the selected collection is stored remotely or when that external source requires account access.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can Cloud Music play local files?

Yes. The app includes a local music library and device scanning workflow.

Does it organize artists and playlists?

Yes. Artists and playlists are available as dedicated library views.

Can I listen offline?

Compatible music already stored on the device can be played from the local library without opening a cloud source. Remote collections require network access.

Does Cloud Music provide a subscription catalog?

Cloud Music is designed around music you already store locally or access through a source you configure, rather than a bundled subscription catalog.

Where can I install Cloud Music?

Cloud Music is available for Android through Google Play.
