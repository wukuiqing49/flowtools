Product purpose

What GeoLens helps you do

GeoLens is designed for field photography where an image needs more context than a timestamp. It combines capture or import, structured metadata, optional location information and a configured delivery path in one Android workspace.

Templates help teams repeat the same descriptive fields across jobs, while the workbench keeps current records and settings visible. This makes GeoLens suitable for industrial documentation, site evidence and other photo workflows that need traceable context.

Capture and metadata preparation can take place at the job site, including environments with limited connectivity. A network connection is required when the workflow sends prepared media to a configured server or another remote destination.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is GeoLens designed for?

GeoLens is designed for industrial and professional field photography where location and metadata matter.

Can it include location information?

Yes. Location can be included when you enable the relevant Android permission and workflow.

Can GeoLens be used offline?

Capture and metadata preparation can be performed in field conditions with limited connectivity. Uploading to a configured server requires a network connection.

Where are photos uploaded?

Direct delivery uses the destination configured for the job. That server's authentication, access and retention rules also apply.

Where can I install GeoLens?

GeoLens is available for Android through Google Play.
