Product purpose

What SiteReport helps you do

SiteReport turns a field inspection into a structured record that can be reviewed and shared. Checklists, photo evidence and optional GPS context stay connected to the inspection instead of being scattered across notes, camera folders and separate documents.

After the site work is complete, the collected information can be prepared as a PDF report. This supports repeatable inspections where the final deliverable needs to show what was checked, what was observed and which evidence belongs to each record.

Inspection records and selected media are designed to stay on the device while the field work is in progress, supporting sites with limited connectivity. Network access may be required later when a PDF or project output is shared through an external destination.

Inspection projects and media are designed to remain on the device until you choose to export or share them. Location access is optional and should be enabled only for inspections that require geographic context.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can SiteReport work offline?

Inspection checklists, project records and photo collection are designed for field work where connectivity may be limited. Sharing through an external destination may require a network.

What can I include in a report?

Workflows can include checklists, photos, GPS context and generated PDF reports.

Which inspections can use SiteReport?

Common workflows include property visits, construction punch lists, safety checks and facilities maintenance records.

Is location required?

No. GPS context is optional and should be enabled only for inspections where location is relevant.

Where can I install SiteReport?

SiteReport is available for Android through Google Play.
